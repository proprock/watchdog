# WD-172: bounded ingestion batches

Implemented before WD-149, retaining the spool-to-inbox-to-store path. The change
requires no database migration and preserves receipt fingerprints, FULL SQLite
synchronization, quota admission, and dirty-state recovery. Content compression
(WD-157), analysis recomputation (WD-162), and periodic maintenance scheduling
(WD-152) remain separate changes.

## Transaction and recovery contract

`Inbox.drain` reads at most 100 candidates by default, in chunks of at most 25.
An optional cooperative time budget is checked between chunks. The default
retains the existing 100-candidate reader window without a deadline. Each chunk
measures disk headroom once under the admission lock and conservatively reserves the existing
per-event database/WAL allowance. Insufficient headroom rolls the chunk back and
uses ordinary single-record admission so reclamation stays outside the batch.

Events, receipts, fact projections, and session states commit together. Codex
state is replayed once per affected identity per chunk. A failed transaction or
projection restores the in-memory state cache too. Inputs are acknowledged after
commit, so interruption before acknowledgement replays committed UUIDs without
inserting another event.

`tests/test_ingestion_batch.py` checks six commits and six Codex replays for 55
events with chunks of ten; real child-process termination before commit and
before unlink for 20 events; rollback on a second-insert failure and on projection
failure; duplicate/conflicting receipts; projection equivalence after reopen;
quota fallback and refusal; and resumable time-budgeted chunks advancing 5/5/2
records across three calls.

## Measurement

The opt-in `scripts/benchmark_ingestion.py` takes a read-only SQLite backup of an
explicit source database, then clones that same seed for each run. It does not
call providers or change live configuration. Public output contains only counts,
timings, a seed hash, and platform information. Synthetic events form one Codex
session, exercising repeated state projection as the session grows.

Throughput runs use 2,000 events without instrumentation; separate profiles use
200 events. Each reader window admits at most 100 raw spool records and drains at
most 100 inbox candidates, matching the daemon's ingestion order without its
poll sleeps, transcript enrichment, maintenance, or findings refresh. Raw spool
publication and inbox ingestion are timed separately. The baseline uses the
original single-event admission/commit/replay path through
`batch_size=1`; the changed path uses 25. The raw spool and intermediate inbox
remain in both variants. This is a synchronous backlog benchmark, not a live
provider latency measurement or a claim to reproduce the historical two-day
backlog's exact traffic mix.

Results and final verification are recorded with the measurement in
[verification.md](../verification.md) and [wd172-ingestion.json](wd172-ingestion.json).
